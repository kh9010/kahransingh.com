Option A

Club Med Kani

Sumi's quote, via Chandan

₹1,31,000 per adult, twin share

4 nights all-inclusive · Superior Room Beachfront · includes speedboat transfers, green tax, GST, TCS, travel insurance.

Quoted 23–27 Nov — but Thanksgiving (Thu 26) anchors our week, so we've asked for a revised 25–29 Nov quote. Pending from Sumi.

What all-inclusive covers (verified)

  • Three daily buffets with a live Indian station
  • All-day open bar — house wines, beers, spirits, cocktails
  • Kids clubs, ages 4–17
  • Sailing, kayak, snorkeling, SUP, trapeze, padel, yoga
  • Nightly shows
  • Paid extras: premium labels, scuba, spa, excursions

Getting there

25–30 min speedboat from Malé airport; boats run late (one guest crossed at midnight) — no dark-water lockout.

The honest read

  1. The renovation window

    The overwater-suite zone is being renovated 1 Aug–18 Dec 2026, with construction noise 9am–6pm daily — our dates sit inside that window. Our beachfront rooms are in a different zone, but it needs a straight answer from the resort.

  2. The age-4 cutoff

    Included kids clubs start at age 4 — the 2-year-old's childcare is a paid extra. (The brochure's "4 months–17 years" line overstates it.)

  3. The competing quotes are in — and it's the value pick

    The agent's 5-star islands (Option C) came back ~25–47% higher per adult, so the WOW rate is now looking like the best-priced of the lot. Still worth locking the revised 25–29 Nov dates before signing.

Option B

Meeru Island Resort

The strongest all-inclusive we turned up on our own research — a bigger island that pushes the idea even further (its "Dine Around" plan puts no prices on any bill). The catch: it's the one option here still without a live price.

Verified strengths

  • The all-inclusive Dine Around plan's signed bills carry no prices at all, and nothing lands on the room account — the resort's own policy document says so
  • Stocked minibar refilled daily — beer, wine, soft drinks, snacks — included
  • Bar snacks 11:00–23:00
  • Kids playroom from age 3 — Koamas, ages 3–12
  • Island zoned family-side / adults-side, with two pools, two buffets, two spas
  • No known construction over our dates

Getting there

55-min speedboat from Malé — half an hour more than Kani, still no seaplane.

The honest read

  1. Koamas is a playroom, not a programme

    Supervised sessions, not Club Med's full-day programme.

  2. The only one still unpriced

    Every other option here now has a real number on it; Meeru still needs a live late-Nov quote. Likely a similar band to Club Med, but unconfirmed — worth pricing before it earns a spot or drops off.

  3. Buffet depth, unconfirmed

    The buffet's Indian/vegetarian depth is unconfirmed — one question to the resort.

  4. Water villas are adults-only

    Under-18s can't stay in water villas — we'd be in beach rooms anyway.

Also close

Sun Siyam Olhuveli

45-min speedboat, 24/7 all-inclusive with an included Indian restaurant (Namaste), kids club from 3. More small exclusions on the ground (a couple of restaurants and "signature" dishes cost extra), which is why Meeru edges it.

The honest read

  1. On price, Club Med still leads

    Converted (table above), the agent's 5-star islands run ~25–47% more per adult than Club Med's WOW rate — so Kani is looking well-priced, and the real question becomes what that premium buys.

  2. Kurumba is the family-logistics pick

    A 10-minute transfer with a 2-year-old and a group of twenty is worth real money — and it's the cheapest of the agent three, closest to Club Med on price, with the widest choice of restaurants.

  3. Varu is the most premium plate

    Premium drinks, a massage each, manta trips — but its à-la-carte speciality allowance scales with length of stay, so four nights only unlocks a slice of it, and it's the top of the price band.

  4. Per-room, not per-head

    The £ prices are whole rooms (2 adults, or 2 adults + a child); the ₹ column halves them for comparison. For the real party — several rooms, multiple kids, Delhi/US flights not London — Amit needs our exact rooming to firm it up.

Getting there + weather

The flight, honestly

Delhi → Malé~4h25mNonstop on Air India; November is one of the route's cheapest months.
Seattle / NYC → Malé~23h+One-stop — same order as getting to Thailand.

The honest weather note: Late November is the tail of the Maldives wet season (it formally runs to early December) — most days are fine, but brief showers are more likely than on Phi Phi, where late Nov opens the dry season. Worth knowing, not worth panicking about.
