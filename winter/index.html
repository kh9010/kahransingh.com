For those who can stay longer

Extend your stay

The core is the four nights; the rest fly home on the 29th. But the Golden Triangle is well placed for an add‑on — easiest for anyone already in India or with a flexible week.

Top pick

Luang Prabang, Laos

+2–3 nts1h flight from Chiang MaiDry in NovAll ages

A tiny UNESCO town where the Mekong meets the mountains: dawn alms, the turquoise pools of Kuang Si Falls, sunset from Mount Phousi, an elephant sanctuary. Slow and soulful — perfect for grandparents and kids alike. The natural extension, and an easy one.

Bangkok

+2–3 ntsOn the route home

The easiest bolt‑on — everyone connects through it. Grand Palace and Wat Pho early, longtail canals, rooftops, IconSiam for the teens. A day or two is plenty.

Andaman coast

+3–4 ntsKrabi / Phang NgaDry side in Nov

End soft and horizontal. The Andaman side is sunny in late Nov (the Gulf isn't): limestone cliffs, island‑hopping, warm water. Skip Phuket — we've done it.

Also possible for the few with real time: carry on to Japan (an easy hop from Bangkok, following the family already heading there).

Good to know

The practical bits

· Weather

Cool, dry, ~18–28°C. A light layer for jungle mornings.

· Bring

Walking shoes, swimwear, temple cover (shoulders & knees), a hat.

· Visas

Visa‑free / e‑visa for Indian & US passports. Steps to follow once booked.

· Time

GMT+7 — 1½h ahead of India, ~12h ahead of the US East Coast.

· Money

Thai baht; cards everywhere. Anantara is largely all‑inclusive.

· Kids

Cribs, nannies, kids' clubs standard at both. Under‑12s free at Anantara.
