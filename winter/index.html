The island

Two small islands, a wooden jetty, and a reef you can swim to.

Sangeli sits out on the northwest edge of North Malé Atoll — under an hour by speedboat from the airport, no seaplane. The boat is ours, and it's included.

The main island holds the villas, the pools and the kids club. A jetty walks you across to One Banyan, the quieter adults-only island with its own pool and the two à-la-carte restaurants. The house reef starts off the beach, so snorkelling is a morning swim, not an excursion.

Everything is on the sand. Every villa has a terrace and a few steps to the water; nobody is on a boardwalk over the lagoon. A hundred and thirty-seven villas in all — small enough to feel like ours for the week.

The kitchen is a point of pride: a live-cooking buffet that changes theme every night, a big share of Indian chefs, and a team that takes special requests at the table.

The week

Five days, loosely.

A sketch, not a schedule. The only fixed thing is Thursday's table.

  1. Tuesday 24Arrive

    Land in Malé, walk to the jetty, and the speedboat does the rest. Find your villa, find the water, find each other. Drinks on the suite's terrace before the first dinner at the Courtyard.

  2. Wednesday 25Settle in

    First reef swim off the beach. Kids club opens its doors; the divers start in the pool. Sunset fishing from the dhoni for anyone who wants to bring something back for the chefs.

  3. Thursday 26Thanksgiving

    A slow day by design. Naps on One Banyan, the kids in the pool, and in the evening one long table for all of us — we're asking the island to set it.

  4. Friday 27Out on the water

    Boat day: a sandbank, a dolphin run, a snorkel spot beyond the reef — the excursions are part of the plan. Spa slots in the afternoon. First à-la-carte dinner across the jetty.

  5. Saturday 28Last full day

    The divers do their open-water dives. Everyone else does whatever they missed. Second à-la-carte dinner, or the Saturday theme night, or both ends of the same evening.

  6. Sunday 29Home

    An early boat back to Malé for the midday Delhi flight; a later one for everyone else. Same jetty, slightly browner.

Every age

Something for the three-year-old and for the seventy-six-year-old.

  1. The little ones — 3, 5 and 8

    A kids club with its own pool, shallow warm water off the beach, and cousins to chase. The two older ones are in from day one; we've asked the island to make sure the youngest joins his brother.

  2. The divers

    A proper PADI centre on the island. Two of us want our open-water certification — do the theory online before we fly and the four dives fit inside the week.

  3. The grandparents

    Beach villas on the sand, a short flat walk to everything, a speedboat rather than a seaplane, and a quiet island across the jetty when the pool gets loud.

  4. The ones who want to do nothing

    One Banyan is adults-only by day: a pool, a bar, a book, and a spa treatment with your name on it.

Before you book flights

Five small things.

  • Check your name reached us as it appears on your passport (and the kids' dates of birth) — a quick message to Kahran or Pradeep if you're not sure.
  • Land in Malé on Tuesday 24 by mid-afternoon if you can; leave Sunday 29 any time after late morning.
  • Passports valid six months past the trip; Indian and US passports get a visa on arrival.
  • Divers: finish the PADI eLearning before you fly, so the week is water, not classroom.
  • Pack reef-safe sunscreen, rash guards for the kids, a hat — and one nice outfit for Thursday night.
