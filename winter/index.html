The idea

Why this one

We're an active, curious family who like to walk, hike and explore — so the trip should be too. Northern Thailand is the rare place that's genuinely thrilling for every age at once, easy enough to reach from both Delhi and the US, and somewhere we haven't gathered before.

Late November is the sweet spot: the start of the north's cool, dry season — clear skies, mild days. We keep it to two bases over four nights, with a single gentle travel morning in the middle, so the toddlers and grandparents are never on a long road.

What we're solving for

The brief, in order

  • Easy to get to — works for the Delhi twelve and the US eight (with two little ones flying long‑haul).
  • All together — one resort, one table, everyone under the same roof as much as possible.
  • Active — we move. This is what ruled out a sit‑still beach trip.
  • Every generation — real adventure for the kids, independence for the nineteen‑year‑olds, ease and comfort for the elders.
  • Somewhere new — last time was Phuket; this is a different Thailand entirely.
Where we'd stay

Two bases

2 nights Chiang Mai · 2 nights Golden Triangle

Four Seasons Chiang Mai

Mae Rim valley · multi‑bedroom residences

Set in working rice paddies fifteen minutes from town. We take adjoining residences so we're all together, with pools, a cooking school, temples nearby, and a night market for the older kids. The #1‑rated resort in Thailand — and very easy on the grandparents.

Anantara Golden Triangle Elephant Camp

Where Thailand, Laos & Myanmar meet · ~10‑room block

The showstopper. A small jungle resort built around an elephant camp: walk‑your‑own‑elephant mahout mornings, hikes to three‑country viewpoints, Mekong longtail boats, and glass "jungle bubbles" you can sleep in beside the elephants. Largely all‑inclusive, so once we're there, everything's handled.

Also on the table

Two strong alternatives

Each makes a different trade against the brief. Thailand still edges it for our dates — but here's the honest case for the other two.

Option BBali — one villa, all of us under one roof

The "never leave the compound" answer: a single 10‑plus‑bedroom estate, everyone together for the whole week, not a single internal move. Wonderful — but late November is the start of Bali's wet season, and the best single‑roof estates lean more pool‑and‑beach than hike‑and‑explore. Best if "one house, one week" is the thing that matters most.

Option CVietnam — the grand journey, north to south

The most adventurous of the three: Hanoi's old quarter, a night on a Halong Bay junk, the lantern streets of Hoi An, on down toward Ho Chi Minh. And it has the easiest flight from Delhi of all — direct, about 4½ hours. Two catches for our window, though: it's a three‑ or four‑base journey with several internal hops rather than one home — a lot of moving with a 2‑ and 4‑year‑old — and late November is peak rainy season on the central coast, with Hoi An (the marquee stop) prone to flooding. The north and south shine in November; the middle doesn't. It's a spectacular 10‑day trip waiting for a drier month — or, trimmed to just the north, a calm and dry few days, but then it isn't really "north to south."
