27 · The years hold the block

18 d's makings, 19 b's years, 25 d's lift, as one page. 194 facts, 35 at their own year, 159 placed by a recorded reason.

  2. b19 b's rows: year, lanes, the run, and the making lifted beside its year
  3. cthe makings large, the years a small dense ground beside them
  4. d18 d's block whole, the years a spine down its side
