45 · The stone, with a way in

The stone stays the home, as it is: the photograph and the poem broken across it, every facet giving something, the doors, the six ideas, the chain, the years. These are ways of bringing a stranger into it. In all four the poem is set larger and straight on a slightly deeper ground, by the stone's own dials.

  1. ait says what it is: who this is and what the page does, on the photograph whole; then the photograph is cut into the stone
  2. bit shows what it holds: asked, every facet says what is behind it, and the door lines say where they lead
  3. cit opens one for you: once, after arriving, a facet opens by itself, then an idea, and both close
  4. da voice walks with you: one line by the name says what just happened and how much is left
  5. allthe four together: said, cut, shown once, then walked with; the key waits to be asked
