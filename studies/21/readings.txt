# Study 21 — the readings. Hand judgements, read by build.py.
# Each poem: its slug, then candidate door lines in order of how strongly they hold an idea.
# A line is:   phrase | concept concept ...      (\n inside a phrase = a line break in the poem)
# The concepts are the fine grain (CONCEPTS in build.py); each vocabulary groups them into its own ideas.
# A line starting "  note:" is a note on the poem: where the ideas strain against it.
