about

Hello. I’m Kahran.

I write poems, and most days I build software. The poems came first. The software is newer, and it is mostly tools to support myself: one place where everything I leave behind lands, a day planned from it, and a public record of what got built. The photograph and poem on the home page come out of the same machine, one of each a day.

I run a storytelling studio, Joyus, with Divya Tak. I invest early with Sweta and Sahil at Tres Monos Capital, sit on the board of TomboyX, and keep a few standing slots for founders in the middle of something that matters. Before that I ran Vidyanext in Bangalore. When it could not be saved, I placed all 65+ people in two months.

I shoot on a Leica Q2 Monochrome. I’m learning Kathak from Rachna Nivas in New York. I’m married to Gourav: we eloped in Telluride, in the snow, and did it again in Jaisalmer with 150 people a year later.

Write to me. Tell me what’s going on.
