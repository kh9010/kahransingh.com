25 · The lift and the rabbit hole

23 c and d as one. 203 of 206 facts now have kin, each with its reason. Where do the jumps and the trail live?

  1. athe fact lifts out carrying its jumps; its kin light in the block; the trail rides on top
  2. bthe fact lifts out; its kin, lit in the block, are the jumps; the trail stays marked in the block
  3. ca column of lifted facts beside the block, newest open, the way back down it
  4. dthe kin gather inside the lift, by reason, and the block stays still
