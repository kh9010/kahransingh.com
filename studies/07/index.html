companies

Joyus is a storytelling and experience design studio with Divya Tak. Tres Monos invests early in underrepresented founders, with Sweta Jagirdar and Sahil Sagar. At Vidyanext, in Bangalore, I took over after a leadership crisis; when it could not be saved, I placed all 65+ people within two months.

Between those, a few standing slots for founders in the middle of something that matters.

Both with Divya Tak. Poem Films sets each poem against two unlike pictures and was selected for SEEN V by Creativity Alive Boulder. The Line Before is a touchscreen where visitors assemble a poem from 230 fragments by choosing between emotional poles, submitted to Science Gallery Bengaluru.
