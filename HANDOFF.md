## Version 2 in detail
- **2026-10-28, Study 13, the walk as a thread** (`studies/13/`). `photography.html` copied after the aspect fix (same CSS, walk, pause rules, lightbox), plus:
  - `features.py` reads the live catalogue (`allPhotos` in `/photography.html`, so the study can never walk a different set), opens each file EXIF-turned, and measures a 128px copy into `features.json`: tone (Lab lightness, contrast), colour (Lab a/b, chroma, 12-bin hue histogram, mono flag from pixels, not from the camera tag: two "Q2" files are monochrome), lines (edge energy horizontal / vertical / two diagonals, density), horizon (height of the strongest horizontal band and its strength), layout (8x8 lightness grid; centroid of the dark mass), subject, place and hour (from the site's photo scoring since the reuse pass below; first cut used alt text and the home's captions). No model, no network; `--check` exits 1 if the json would change.
  - `chain.js` (browser and node): per-feature distances each divided by their median over all pairs, a weighted mean of them, then the open path that keeps neighbours close: nearest-neighbour from every start, the best kept, 2-opt. A step is named by the feature that carries it most (weight x closeness) if one is under `link-min`, else shown as a dot. Colour never names two monochromes.
  - Orders: **colour** = blend weights with colour and tone x `lead`; **line** = lines, horizon and layout x `lead`; **blend** = the weights as set; **today** = a fresh shuffle, named under the blend weights so a random step's (lack of) reason is visible too.
  - Dials (`defaults.json`, `?tune` via `node studies/tune-server.cjs`): the seven weights (`w-colour` 1, `w-tone` 1, `w-lines` 1, `w-horizon` 1, `w-layout` 1, `w-subject` 1.5, `w-place` 0.5), `lead` 3, `link-min` 0.4, `pair-every` 5 (0 = no diptychs), `strip-h` 44px, `link-size` .7rem. A moved order dial reloads the page after the save so the chain is recomputed.
  - Numbers at the defaults (mean neighbour distance, chain vs a shuffle, 1 = as alike as two pictures usually are; steps named): colour 0.66 vs 1.05, 64/70; line 0.68 vs 1.03, 62/70; blend 0.64 vs 1.00, 66/70. `link-min` is 0.4, not 0.6, because at 0.6 a random shuffle still found a reason for 44 of 70 steps (a name meant little); at 0.4 a shuffle gets about 27, the chains 62–66. Blend scores closest because the alt-text subject words (weight 1.5, a 0 when shared) carry it; its runs (eight indoors in a row) are tidy in words and loose to the eye, which is why the pick is line. `verify` requires every chain under 0.75x a shuffle and 56 of 70 steps named; watched red by making `path` return a scrambled order (colour 1.05 vs 1.05).

A local image embedding (CLIP-like) would give "a doorway to a doorway"; none is installed here (no torch, and kMini's ollama was not reachable from this session), so it was left out rather than half-done.
  - **Colour, re-tuned for mid-tone transitions** (2026-10-28, Kahran: "I want it by color. I love the mid tone transitions"). The colour order adds three terms to its weighted mean (`dist` in `chain.js`): a lightness step costs `l-step` x (dL/15)^2 and a chroma step `c-step` x (dC/10)^2, squared so a gradual shift is nearly free and a jump is dear; then a step whose two pictures are both mid-tone (L in `mid-lo`..`mid-hi`, 28..56, falling off over 10 L) is discounted by up to `mid-weight`, more the gentler its lightness step. Defaults `mid-weight` 0.5, `l-step` 0.5, `c-step` 0.3, picked by sweeping 27 settings and reading the thread: from bright greys, through a long mid-tone run (forests, streets, flowers, interiors at one tone), down into the night pictures, with no dark-to-pale jump. 44 of the 71 are monochrome, so for most pictures colour means tone. Numbers, old weights then new (`Chain.colourStats`, with the scoring's notes and hour in place): mean neighbour distance on colour + tone alone 0.68 then 0.71 (the order trades some hue closeness for a smoother tone line); mean lightness step 7.3 then 3.0, chroma step 4.7 then 2.3; steps of more than 30 L 0 then 0; mid-tone to mid-tone steps (both in the band, dL <= 8, dC <= 6) 13 then 34 of 70; the 9 pale pictures (L >= 56) in 4 runs then 1. Under its own weights the chain is 0.61 apart against 2.33 for a shuffle (the squared terms make a shuffle dear). Before the scoring swap the same comparison read 17 then 34, 4 runs then 2.
  - **The graph** (`?view=graph`, any order): nodes are 96px copies in `studies/13/thumbs/`, written by `features.py` (EXIF-turned, `--check` fails if one is missing), placed by lightness and by warm/cool (0.35 a + 0.9 b, square-rooted; monochromes on the grey line), then settled by d3-force so none covers another. Edges join pairs under `edge-max` (0.45, about 90 edges) on the colour order's distance; the path is the walk of the order on screen. The band is the mid-tone range. Phone: lightness runs down the long side, tap a picture to light it, tap again or the empty field to let go; desktop: hover. The line above the graph reads step number, the word in, the picture's notes from the scoring, the word out, and its L (and C if it has colour).

## Wrong turns worth knowing
- **The first overflow check could not fail.** `scrollHeight > clientHeight` on a tile missed text that was plainly spilling out, because the tile is a centred flex column and overflow above the top edge is not counted. Caught by stuffing a long line into one tile and watching the check stay green. It now measures the name and parts boxes against the tile's padding box.
- **The flow view at rest shows no wall.** Since the door rework the tiles exist only in the flow view (`.tools-layer:not(.is-flow) .wall-block { display: none }`), so `WALL_ROWS` and the lean only matter for the flight's starting state. - **The first height ruling passed on the live page.** It compared the page's height at first paint with its height after 10 s of walking; in 10 s the walk reaches two pictures and the lazy ones below never load, so the live page grew under 2% and the check proved nothing.
