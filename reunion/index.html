Questions

The fine print (there barely is any)

Can I bring my partner / kids?

Afternoon: yes, absolutely — it's a park, it's built for it. Evening: partners welcome, kids stay home.

What does it cost?

Nothing. The picnic is covered. In the evening you buy your own drinks.

I live far away. Is it worth flying in?

Twenty years only happens once. Come for the evening alone if that's what travel allows — out-of-towners get priority hugs.

I only sort of graduated with you / I was '05 or '07.

If you were part of our world at ICS, you're welcome. Bring yourself.

This page is the loop. RSVP with your email and updates come to you.
