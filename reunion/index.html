The shape of the day

Five hours at one long table, then whatever happens next

No picnic-then-drive-downtown, no schedule, no one making a speech. We have the tables from 2 until 7 and the patio is ten steps from the water. That is the entire plan.

2 pm – 7 pm

The long table

Two or three of Moss Bay Hall's big communal tables on the patio, held for us for five straight hours. Platters keep arriving, the lake sits right there, and you finally hear what actually happened senior year. Wander off, come back, bring the kids — it's an afternoon, not a seating.

Where: the patio, Moss Bay Hall · 111 Central Way booked
7 pm onward

The stragglers

At 7 they need the tables back — August, Friday, packed patio, fair enough. Whoever is still going moves inside to the bar, which is first-come-first-served and has never once been a problem for a group that has had five hours of practice. Fly in for just this stretch if that's all travel allows: it counts.

Where: the bar, same building — you walk about thirty feet no booking needed

Questions

The fine print (there barely is any)

How do I RSVP?
Right on this page, at the top — start typing your name, it finds you, tap a couple of buttons. No login, no app. Even a "can't make it" helps.
Can my photo be current instead of 2006?
Sure — email Kahran a photo you like and it goes up. Nothing gets pulled from anywhere without you handing it over.
Is the date final?
Yes. Friday, August 28, 2026, 2 pm, Moss Bay Hall. Booked, done, go ahead and buy the flight.
Why a Friday and not the Saturday?
Two reasons, and the second is the real one. The Hall could only give us the long afternoon on a Friday — a Saturday would have been three hours, or a venue fee. But also: a Saturday swallows the whole weekend. This way you give up one afternoon and keep both weekend days, which matters if you already have something on. If a Friday genuinely rules you out, tell me — that's worth knowing even now.
Where is it, exactly?
Moss Bay Hall, 111 Central Way, downtown Kirkland — on the water. We have two or three of their long communal tables on the patio.
Is it a private room?
No, and it's better that way. It's the open patio with our tables in the middle of it, other people around, August in Kirkland doing its thing. Nobody has to whisper.
What happens at 7?
We give the tables back — the patio is slammed on an August Friday and that's the deal that got us five hours with no venue fee. Whoever's still going walks inside to the bar. In practice this is the good part.
Can I bring my partner / kids?
Yes — bring them. It's a patio by the lake on a summer afternoon, which is about as kid-tolerant as this gets.
What does it cost?
Nothing to come. The food is covered; drinks go on your own tab.
I live far away. Worth flying in?
Twenty years only happens once. And a Friday afternoon start means you can fly in that morning and still have the whole weekend in Seattle after.
I was '05 or '07.
If you were part of our world at ICS, you're welcome.
My name on this page is wrong (or I want it off).
Email Kahran — swapped, fixed, or gone the same day, no questions asked.
I'm not on Facebook — how do I stay in the loop?
This page is the loop; it's updated as things change. RSVP with your email and updates come to you too.
Who's organizing this?
Kahran Singh — yes, the same one who organized everything in 2006. Some things don't change. Want to co-conspire? See "Own one thing" above.
