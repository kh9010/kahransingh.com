Questions

The fine print (there barely is any)

How do I RSVP?
Right on this page, at the top — start typing your name, it finds you, tap a couple of buttons. No login, no app. Even a "can't make it" helps.
Can my photo be current instead of 2006?
Sure — email Kahran a photo you like and it goes up. Nothing gets pulled from anywhere without you handing it over.
Is Aug 29 final?
Yes — that's the date. Come make a weekend of it.
Can I bring my partner / kids?
Afternoon: yes, absolutely. Evening: partners welcome, kids stay home.
What does it cost?
Nothing. The picnic is covered; in the evening you buy your own drinks.
I live far away. Worth flying in?
Twenty years only happens once. Come for the evening alone if that's what travel allows.
I was '05 or '07.
If you were part of our world at ICS, you're welcome.
My name on this page is wrong (or I want it off).
Email Kahran — swapped, fixed, or gone the same day, no questions asked.
I'm not on Facebook — how do I stay in the loop?
This page is the loop; it's updated as things change. RSVP with your email and updates come to you too.
Who's organizing this?
Kahran Singh — yes, the same one who organized everything in 2006. Some things don't change. Want to co-conspire? See "Own one thing" above.
