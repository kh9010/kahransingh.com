Bladder bag
in response to Warming by jos charles
scroll
I, bladder bag, am made cold again, my secret inner fire being doused again, again, again, where did that warmth that surrounded me once go. Who she this whale I once was disgorging my contents and purging my sisters to make a slop, what is this soup She this whale I once was takes me with a soft hand, speaks gently, makes soft the water and stirs away the hard pieces that come of the night. I am made cold and made warm, I, basti, am made anew, I am container of slop and holder of soup, I am reborn as bag.
