Most of my work happens with a few people at a time — founders, mostly, in the middle of something that matters. Here are the shapes it takes.

Counsel

A standing slot — I keep a few at a time

Recurring time with one founder or leader, on whatever’s live that week — strategy, product, the decision you keep circling. Nothing is out of scope, which is rather the point. I’m honest when the slots are full.

The hard moment

Pivots, turnarounds, raises — bounded work

For when the next few months decide the next few years. I once took over a company in the middle of a leadership crisis — and when it couldn’t be saved, placed all 65+ people in two months. This kind of work has a beginning and an end, and we agree on both.

AI, set up as a partner

For individuals & small teams

I’ll build your AI working setup for you — not a demo, the thing you actually use every day. My favorite part is watching someone reach something that felt out of range a week earlier.
