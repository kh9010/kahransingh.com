Step three

Know before you go

Watch

Fires & smoke

Fires have closed trails and roads near the pass; the Enchantments are expected to stay open, but air quality depends on the wind. Check the Okanogan–Wenatchee alerts page and an AQI map the day before we drive. Some rain is in the Leavenworth forecast, which helps.

Required

Permit & camp rules

Our overnight permit covers the Snow Lakes zone: designated hiker campsites at Nada or Snow Lakes only. The named permit holder must be with the group, permit on paper — rangers do check on this trail.

Required

Parking pass

A Northwest Forest Pass (or America the Beautiful) on the dash of every car at Snow Lakes TH. In-and-out route, so no car shuttle needed — just carpools.

Required

No campfires, ever

Stoves only, everywhere in the Enchantments — a hard rule year-round, and doubly so in a smoky August.

Required

Waste

There are well-signed pit toilets throughout (some with mighty fine views), but carry a blue bag from the trailhead dispenser as backup and pack out what the toilets don't take. Everything else walks out with us.

Know

The goats are bold

They crave salt: they'll chew sweaty pack straps and follow anyone who pees near the trail. Pee on bare rock well away from trails and camp, never feed them. Rodents will raid any food left out — hence the canisters.

Know

August up high

Hot sun on bare granite by day, near-freezing possible at camp before dawn. Afternoon thunderstorms happen — start the Core day early. Water is everywhere but treat all of it.

Know

No signal

Phones are cameras up there. Download offline maps before Leavenworth, leave the plan with someone at home, and the satellite communicator on the gear list is our real safety line.
